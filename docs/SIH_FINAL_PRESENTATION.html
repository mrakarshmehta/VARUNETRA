THE URBAN CRISIS

The Problem: Why Traditional Systems Fail

Rapid runoff, variable drainage conduits, and the disconnect between science and field operations

01 Rapid Runoff Transition

Dense urban basins have >70% impervious coverage. Infiltration is negligible, converting localized cloudbursts into street inundation within 15–25 minutes. Regional models are too coarse and slow.

02 Spatial Drainage Head Loss

Conduits feature uneven gradients and river outfall backpressure. When receiving rivers like the Ganga rise, flap gates seal or reverse flow occurs, surcharging road manholes regardless of rainfall volume.

03 The Static Map Fallacy

Static 1-in-100 year flood hazard maps cannot answer: Which road is passable right now for an ambulance? Which sump needs a dewatering pump? Operators need dynamic corridor intelligence.
